Alternative and unstructured information is abundant in Chinese agricultural futures markets, but most of it is noise. This paper uses machine-learning methods to isolate the component that is genuinely priced, and characterises the conditions under which alternative information shocks carry signal.

Status: under review at the *Journal of Futures Markets*.
